# Shore props — art-directed iteration

Reference: user-supplied island screenshots; borrow faceted limestone silhouettes,
stone-ring campsite and warm flame palette. Three reef variants, metre scale,
ground pivots, GLB for Three.js. Reefs cluster on the flanks of the fishing area.
Firepit is 1.7 m across with charcoal bed, crossed logs, 11 enclosing stones and
six stylized flame tongues. No organic character modeling or animation rigs.

Validated in Blender: evaluated triangle counts under 12k each; transforms applied
before export. Multiview blockout, final clay, silhouette and material renders are
saved alongside editable shore_props.blend. Materials are palette-based PBR;
production texture baking and LODs are outside this iteration.
