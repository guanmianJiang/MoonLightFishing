# Distant ferry art brief

Art-directed low-poly cruise and excursion ships for the water beyond the
fishing spots. Borrow the supplied island references' simplified silhouettes
and soft palette: long pointed cream hull, muted teal lower band, stepped
passenger decks, broad blue windows, one terracotta funnel, and a few safety
details. The larger liner gets a raised forward bridge; the smaller ship is
more compact. At the default camera angle both should read as small, distant
vessels at the edges of the water, with slow horizontal movement. Keep the
central casting area clear and avoid fine details that disappear at this size.
The overview camera should not show these vessels beside the dock. Their
world-space routes belong roughly 50 metres offshore and should be composed
against the waterline when the player enters the reed fishing camera.

Blender Z-up, metre units, +X bow-forward, body-centred pivot. Embedded palette
materials only; no production texture or rig claim. The overview looks from
the water toward shore, while fishing aims outward across the sea; route
placement must be judged in the latter view.
