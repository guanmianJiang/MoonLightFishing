# Beach kit art brief

Art-directed iteration using the user-supplied screenshots as visual references only.
Borrow the cream / seafoam striped canvas, warm timber, softly faceted silhouettes,
and relaxed campsite composition. Keep existing fishing gameplay and coastline.

Assets: folding deck chair (0.84 m wide, 1.25 m tall) and beach parasol
(3.3 m diameter, 2.85 m tall). Blender Z-up, metre units, ground-centred
pivots; GLB converts to the existing Three.js Y-up scene. Target under 12k
triangles per asset, a small shared palette, no external texture dependencies.

Primary: reclining continuous canvas sling inside an open folding frame;
shallow domed canopy with alternating cream and muted coral gores.
Secondary: crossed legs, transverse frame ties, pivot hardware, radial ribs,
sliding hub and pole. Small bevels catch light without shiny plastic finishes.
Quiet areas: broad canvas panels. Do not add random grunge or dense microdetail.
Materials are palette-based PBR for this art-directed pass, not baked production
textures. Preserve editable blend, generator, multiview reviews and manifest.
