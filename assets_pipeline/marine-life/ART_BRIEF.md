# Marine life iteration
Art-directed low-poly reef shark (2.8 m body length) and golden shoal fish
(0.65 m body length), modeled with Blender Python. The modular-assets skill
informs review/export only; organic fish are outside its hard-surface scope.
Blue dorsal surface, pale belly, readable dorsal/pectoral/caudal fins.
Editable sources and five-view, clay and silhouette renders are included.
GLB +X forward, Y-up; ground/pivot convention is replaced by body-centre pivot.
Runtime uses continuous elliptical tangent headings and rear-weighted travelling
body deformation. Materials are palette PBR; no baked textures or skeletal rig.
